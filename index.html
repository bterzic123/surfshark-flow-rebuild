What this is

We opened Surfshark the way a new user does, then rebuilt the parts we think are leaving money on the table.

Adapty powers subscriptions for 30,000+ apps, so we get to see what actually moves conversion and ARPU across the market rather than inside one app. We pointed that at Surfshark: installed it, went from the first screen to the paywall and through Other plans, read your App Store listing and your pricing page, and looked for gaps between what you already have and what you show people.

What follows is five ideas, not fifteen. Each one is a hypothesis you can A/B test, and each one is built into a clickable prototype so you can see it rather than imagine it. The prototype uses your design, not ours.

We did not redesign your app

Every screen in the prototype is yours: your dark surfaces and white pill buttons, the teal hero photo, the yellow Save 70% badge, your Home rows with their Enable pills, and your Other plans sheet with its 12 / 6 / 1 month tabs. Only the order changed, plus a few things you already own but do not show at the right moment.

You may have tested some of this

We are working from the outside, so we cannot see your experiment history. If one of these is something you have already run, tell us and we will go deeper on the rest rather than repeat it.
