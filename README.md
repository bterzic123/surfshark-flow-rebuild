# Growth Audit — Surfshark

**Live:** https://bterzic123.github.io/surfshark-flow-rebuild/

Five ideas to unlock more revenue from the traffic Surfshark already has, plus a clickable prototype of the iOS onboarding and paywall rebuilt in Surfshark's own design. Prepared by Adapty, October 2026.

## The five ideas

| # | Idea | Potential outcome |
| :-- | :-- | :-- |
| 01 | Give a second chance when they close the paywall: your one-month plan, no trial, next to the 30-day money-back guarantee | ARPU +10–15% |
| 02 | Let people in before you ask for an account: the account moves to after purchase | CR +8% · ARPU +17% |
| 03 | Ask what they want protected, then show their Home screen already set up | CR +10–20% |
| 04 | Let the paywall name the plan they need: Starter or One, with their picks and devices | CR +15–20% |
| 05 | Put your 4.7 from 133K ratings and your PCMag and CNET awards next to the price | CR +10–15% |

## Grounding

Every price, rating, award and feature line comes from Surfshark's own App Store listing, website, pricing page and in-app screens (euro storefront). Screens shown in German and Japanese were translated by Adapty; store prices stay as published in euros.

Impact ranges are Adapty's expected effect from teardowns and A/B tests across subscription apps — not measured lift for Surfshark.
